## Posting tips

- **Image per tweet:** the 1.7MB logo PNG is too big — make a 1200×675
  card with the FOT logo + a tagline for each tweet. Free tools:
  Canva, Figma. Or use the Statcounter-tracked social share image.
- **First tweet of a long thread gets cut off** on most clients — put
  the punchline in tweet 1, not tweet 8.
- **Post timing:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9-11am US Eastern, 2-4pm UTC.
  Avoid Mondays (people are buried) and Fridays (people are checked
  out).
- **Reply to yourself** with each tweet — don't use a thread app, the
  engagement metrics are lower.
- **Engage in the first 30 minutes:** reply to every comment on the
  thread, quote-retweet people who share, and pin the thread to your
  profile.
- **Don't shill in replies.** People notice. Just answer technical
  questions.
- **Do pin the thread** to your profile for at least 2 weeks.
